Marifort Gate installed.

1. Port-forward the proxy service (restarting wrapper for kind):

  bash tools/helm_port_forward.sh
  # or: kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "rag-protection.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

2. Smoke the deployment:

  curl -s http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/health
  curl -s -X POST http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/v1/query \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer employee-demo-token" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"query":"What are support hours?","top_k":4}'

3. Open the operator console:

  http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/ui

Local Compose-parity (EE image, Auth0 ACL, Drive/.env entitlements):

  bash tools/helm_start.sh
  bash tools/helm_stop.sh
